## 3. Milestone Execution & Revision Protocol

Creative excellence requires iterative refinement, structured within clear operational parameters to maintain production velocity.

### 3.1 Included Revision Rounds
Unless explicitly stated otherwise in your SOW, all fixed-fee project milestones include **two (2) rounds of consolidated revisions**:

- **Round 1 (Structural & Creative Review):** Pacing, narrative flow, layout hierarchy, compositional framing, sound design sync.
- **Round 2 (Micro-Refinement & Polish):** Typography kerning, color grade balancing, minor audio mix tweaks, export format adjustments.

### 3.2 Definition of a Revision vs. Scope Creep
- **Valid Revision:** Adjustments made *within* the approved creative concept, approved script, and established storyboard.
- **Scope Creep / Change Order:** Requesting a completely new concept, changing an approved script after voiceover/animation has started, altering camera angles on rendered 3D scenes, or switching brand styles midway. Scope changes are billed at the Studio's prevailing hourly or milestone rate.

### 3.3 Silent Acceptance SLA (5 Business Days)
Upon delivery of any milestone cut, staging link, or draft export:
- The Client must provide **written, consolidated feedback** within **five (5) business days**.
- If no written feedback or revision notes are submitted within this 5-day window, the milestone deliverable is automatically deemed **Accepted and Approved**, triggering the release of the associated invoice milestone.

### 6.3 Taxes & Deductions
- All fees quoted are exclusive of Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) unless explicitly stated. Applicable GST (18%) will be levied on tax invoices for domestic entities.

### 6.4 Late Payment Penalties
Invoices unpaid after **fifteen (15) calendar days** from the issue date will accrue interest at the rate of **1.5% per month** (18% per annum) or the maximum legal rate, compounded monthly, along with reimbursement of any legal recovery costs incurred by TDDS.

## 7. Project Suspension, Dormancy & Kill Fees

### 7.1 Client-Induced Dormancy (Project Hold)
If a project is halted or delayed by more than **fourteen (14) consecutive calendar days** due to Client delays in providing feedback, assets, or milestone approvals:
- TDDS reserves the right to release reserved production crew, animators, and computing resources.
- Re-activating the dormant project will require a **15% Project Rescheduling Fee** and will be subject to studio schedule availability.

### 7.2 Project Cancellation & Kill Fee
If the Client terminates an active engagement for convenience before completion:
1. TDDS shall retain all deposits and advance payments received to date.
2. The Client shall pay for all completed milestones and prorated work-in-progress up to the official termination notice date.
3. A **Kill Fee equal to 25% of the remaining contract balance** will apply to compensate the studio for lost project booking capacity and preempted scheduling.

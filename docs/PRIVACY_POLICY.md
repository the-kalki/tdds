Your data is processed strictly within the confines of our business relationship:

1. **Service Delivery & Production:** Processing creative media, reviewing rough cuts, staging revisions, and compiling production deliverables.

## 4. Proprietary Asset Confidentiality & NDA Standards

Creative production often involves pre-launch brand secrets, unreleased product launches, and proprietary raw media. We treat all client assets with enterprise-grade operational hygiene:

- **Strict Internal Access:** Raw client media and unreleased project files are restricted strictly to the designated creative director, motion designers, and developers assigned to your project.
- **Mutual Non-Disclosure (NDA):** Every formal commercial contract includes embedded NDA clauses protecting embargoed releases, unreleased product specs, and proprietary campaign concepts.
- **Secure Cloud Storage:** Digital production assets are staged in encrypted, authenticated cloud workspaces (access-controlled Google Workspace / AWS S3 buckets).
- **Post-Delivery Archival:** Raw working files are retained in our offline or cold storage archive for a standard retention window of **180 days** post-project completion to support emergency file retrieval, after which they may be purged unless a long-term retainer is active.

## 6. Cookies & Client-Side Storage

Our website architecture is built with an anti-bloat philosophy:

- **Zero Tracking Pixels:** We do not embed third-party surveillance scripts (e.g., Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel) that track your activity across unrelated websites.
- **Essential Session State:** We only utilize native browser memory for essential UI states (such as active navigation section, drawer toggle state, and reduced-motion user preferences).
- **No Third-Party Advertising Cookies:** You will not be retargeted with display ads across the web after browsing TDDS.

## 8. Your Legal Rights (DPDP Act & International Standards)

Subject to applicable statutory requirements, clients and visitors possess the following rights regarding their personal data:

1. **Right to Access & Summary:** You may request a plain-text summary of the personal information TDDS holds about you.
2. **Right to Correction & Update:** You may request updates to outdated contact numbers, billing addresses, or company details.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may request that we delete your contact records and uncommissioned inquiry briefs, provided the data is not required for ongoing statutory tax audit compliance.
4. **Right to Revoke Consent:** You may opt out of receiving follow-up creative proposals or marketing mailers at any time.
